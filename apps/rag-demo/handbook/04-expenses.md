# Travel and Expense Reimbursement

Business travel is booked through the travel portal. Economy class is the standard for flights
and second class for trains; business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours.

Receipts must be uploaded to the expense portal within 30 days of the expense. Expenses are
reimbursed within 14 days after approval by the team lead. Meals during business trips are
reimbursed up to 28 euros per day within Germany.

Expenses above 1,000 euros need prior approval from the department head. Gifts to business
partners must not exceed 50 euros per person and must be reported to the compliance officer.
