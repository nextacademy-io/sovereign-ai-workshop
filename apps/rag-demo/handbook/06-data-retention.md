# Data Retention and Deletion

Customer contracts and invoices are retained for 10 years, as required by commercial and tax
law. Applicant data is deleted 6 months after the end of the application process unless the
applicant has agreed to a longer storage period.

Call recordings of the customer service are stored for 90 days and are accessible only to
quality assurance and the legal department. Server logs containing IP addresses are deleted
after 30 days. Backups are kept for 35 days.

Deletion requests from customers are processed within 30 days. The data protection officer
keeps the record of processing activities and reviews it once a year.
