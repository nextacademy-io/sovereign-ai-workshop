# IT Incident Response

Every suspected security incident must be reported to the security team at once, using the
hotline or the security mailbox. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete
anything.

The security team classifies incidents as low, medium or critical. For critical incidents the
on-call security engineer must acknowledge within 15 minutes and the incident manager informs
the management board within 1 hour. Incidents involving personal data are reported to the data
protection officer, who decides within 72 hours of becoming aware whether the supervisory
authority must be notified.

After every critical incident a review is held within 5 working days and its findings are
published internally.
