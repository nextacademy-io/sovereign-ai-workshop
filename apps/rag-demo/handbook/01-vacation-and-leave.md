# Vacation and Leave (Nordlicht Logistik GmbH, version 3)

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Part-time employees
receive a proportional number of days. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next
year and must be taken by 31 March; after that date they expire.

Vacation requests of up to 5 consecutive days are approved by the team lead. Requests longer
than 5 consecutive days also need approval from the department head and must be submitted at
least 4 weeks in advance.

Sick leave must be reported to the team lead before the start of the working day. A medical
certificate is required from the third consecutive day of absence.

Parental leave can be taken in up to three blocks. The employee informs the team lead and
the HR department at least 8 weeks before the planned start of each block.
