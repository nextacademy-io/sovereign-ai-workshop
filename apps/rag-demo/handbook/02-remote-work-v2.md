# Remote Work Policy (version 2, supersedes version 1)

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The days are agreed with the team lead and
recorded in the team calendar. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 on all working days.

Remote work from another EU country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year.
Longer stays abroad, or work from a country outside the EU, require written approval from HR
because of tax and social security implications.

The company provides a laptop, a monitor allowance of 250 euros, and reimburses a monthly
internet flat rate of up to 30 euros. Company laptops must not be used on public computers
or shared with family members.
